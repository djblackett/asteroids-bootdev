Visual Polish Features
Starfield Background - Parallax scrolling stars that move slightly as the player moves, creating depth and immersion

Asteroid Textures/Variations - Different visual styles for asteroids (rocky, icy, metallic) with different colors and patterns

Trails & After-images - Short trails behind fast-moving objects, especially during boost

Mini-map - Small corner display showing asteroid positions relative to player (especially useful in multiplayer)

Kill Streak Notifications - Big flashy text/animations for milestone combos (10x, 20x, etc.)

Gameplay Enhancements
Enemy Ships/UFOs - Occasional hostile ships that shoot back at players, worth bonus points

Wave System - Structured waves with increasing difficulty and brief breathing room between waves

Power-up Magnets - Hold a button to slowly pull nearby power-ups toward you

Time Trial Mode - Survive for X seconds or clear all asteroids as fast as possible

Asteroid Fragment Chains - When you destroy an asteroid, fragments briefly glow and award bonus points if destroyed quickly (chain reaction bonus)

Multiplayer Features
Friendly Fire Toggle - Option to enable/disable shooting the other player

Shared Lives Pool - Co-op mode where both players share a life pool

Competitive Modes - King of the Hill (control center area), Race (first to X points), Last Man Standing

Revive System - Dead player can be revived if surviving player collects a special item

Meta Features
Achievements System - Unlockable achievements displayed on screen (e.g., "Untouchable - Clear 10 asteroids without getting hit")

Unlockable Ship Skins - Different visual styles for player ships earned through achievements

Daily Challenge - Seeded random game with specific conditions, compete on global leaderboard

Replay System - Save and replay best runs

Statistics Screen - Track total playtime, asteroids destroyed, highest combo, etc.

Audio/Juice
Proximity Warning System - Audio cue or visual indicator when asteroids are dangerously close

Dynamic Music - Music intensity increases with combo count or danger level

Voice Callouts - Announcer voice for "Double Kill!", "Mega Combo!", etc.

Quick Win Additions
The easiest "wow factor" additions would be:

Starfield background (visual depth, minimal code)
Kill streak notifications (leverages your existing combo system)
Enemy UFOs (adds variety to gameplay)
Wave system with breathing room (better pacing)
